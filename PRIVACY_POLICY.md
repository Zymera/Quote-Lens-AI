# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 6, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Quote Lens AI** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience. Because our app is built with privacy in mind, the vast majority of your data—including sensitive financial quotes and camera data—never leaves your device. 

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android application.

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It
We adhere to the principle of data minimization, collecting only what is necessary to run the app and manage your subscriptions.

**A. Personal Information (Account Data)**
* **What we collect:** When you create an account, we collect your Email Address.
* **How we use it:** We use this strictly for account creation, authentication, and secure login. This process is securely managed through Google Firebase Authentication.

**B. Subscriptions and Payments**
* **What we collect:** We use Google Play Billing to manage in-app subscriptions. We collect anonymous purchase history to verify your premium status. 
* **How it works:** We do not process or have access to your credit card or payment details; all transactions are securely handled directly by Google Play.

**C. Camera Data and Optical Character Recognition (OCR)**
* **What we request:** We request Camera permissions to enable invoice/document scanning features. 
* **How it works (Local Processing):** **We do not record video, nor do we upload your photos or scanned documents to our servers.** The app utilizes Google's ML Kit Text Recognition to process images and extract text *locally* on your device. Your camera feed remains strictly on your phone.

**D. Financial and Client Data (Quotes)**
* **What you input:** You may input client names, quote amounts, and business details.
* **How it works (Local Storage):** **We do not collect or transmit this data to our servers.** All quotes and financial records are saved locally on your device using a Room SQLite Database. 

**E. Usage Data and Analytics**
* **What we collect:** We collect anonymous, aggregated data on how the app is used (e.g., buttons tapped) and diagnostic data (e.g., crash reports). 
* **How we use it:** We use Google Analytics for Firebase to analyze this data to troubleshoot bugs and improve app performance.

## 3. Third-Party Services
To provide our service, we use trusted third-party providers:
* **Google Play Services & Billing:** Used for core functionality and subscriptions. ([Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
* **Google Firebase Authentication:** Used for user login. ([Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))
* **Google Analytics for Firebase:** Used for usage tracking and crash reporting. ([Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))

## 4. Data Security
By designing the app to store sensitive financial data locally and process camera scans on-device, we inherently protect you from server-side data breaches. However, we strongly recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date to maintain device-level encryption. All data transmitted to Firebase (like your email) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

## 5. Your Data Rights
* **Access and Deletion:** Because your financial and scanned data is stored locally, you can view, edit, or delete it at any time directly within the app. Deleting the app clears this local database. 
* **Account Deletion:** You may request the deletion of your account at any time by contacting us, or by using the in-app account deletion feature.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. 

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
